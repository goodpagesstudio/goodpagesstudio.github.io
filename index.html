How it works

Four simple steps.

Everything happens over email, at your pace.

  1. 1

    You email us

    Tell us who you are, what you do and what you need. Scan the QR code or tap the button and the email is ready to fill in.

  2. 2

    We reply and scope it

    We get back to you quickly, check you're a charity or nonprofit, and agree on a simple site together.

  3. 3

    We build it

    We design and build your site, publish it on GitHub Pages and send you the link to review.

  4. 4

    We follow up

    We confirm it's done, then make changes and follow up until you're happy with it.

What you get

A good website, with no strings attached.

  • Free

    No fees for design, building or hosting. You never get an invoice from us.

  • Simple

    A clear, fast site that works well on phones, focused on what your supporters need to find.

  • Open source

    Every site's code is public on GitHub, so anyone can see it, learn from it or help maintain it.

  • Hosted on GitHub Pages

    Reliable, secure hosting that costs nothing, with a real link you can share right away.

  • Yours to keep

    The site and its content belong to you. Take it anywhere and use your own domain whenever you like.

Who qualifies

If you're doing good, just ask.

We build for registered charities, nonprofits and community groups working for the public good. When your request comes in, we do a quick, generous check of your charity status, and we'd rather say yes than look for reasons to say no.

  • Registered charities and nonprofits
  • Volunteer-run and grassroots community groups
  • Small organizations with no website yet, or one that needs a refresh

Not sure if you qualify? Email us anyway and we'll talk it through.
